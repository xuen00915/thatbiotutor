What is Pure/G3 Biology?

What Exactly Is O Level Pure/G3 Biology?

O Level Pure Biology, syllabus 6093, is being renamed G3 Biology starting in 2027. It is a full O Level science for Sec 3 and Sec 4 students.

Unlike Combined Science, Pure Biology covers the complete Cambridge syllabus and counts as a full subject grade for L1R5/L1R4.

Across Sec 3 and Sec 4

Students cover cells, movement of substances, biomolecules, enzymes, human nutrition and transport, respiration, excretion, homeostasis, coordination, plant biology, ecology, molecular genetics, reproduction, and inheritance.

Why students struggle

Four Common Pure/G3 Biology Problems

1. Too much content to memorise

Hundreds of terms, definitions, and processes make brute-force memorisation unreliable under exam conditions.

2. Open-ended questions feel unpredictable

Paper 2 marking is strict, yet answering technique is rarely taught explicitly.

3. Topics feel disconnected

Without seeing how systems connect, application questions become much harder.

4. Hands-on practice is limited

Too little laboratory practice can make the 20% Paper 3 component feel like a gamble.
