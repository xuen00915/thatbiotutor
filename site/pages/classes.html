What is Pure/G3 Biology?

What exactly is O Level
Pure/G3 Biology?

O Level Pure Biology, syllabus 6093 (renamed to G3 Biology starting 2027) is a full-subject O Level science taken by Sec 3 and Sec 4 students. Unlike Combined Science, Pure Bio students are examined on the complete Cambridge syllabus — making it more rigorous but also worth a full subject grade on the L1R5/L1R4 score.

Sec 3 Topics:

Cells · Movement of substances · Biomolecules · Enzymes · Nutrition in humans · Transport in humans · Respiration · Excretion · Homeostasis · Nervous system and the Eye

Sec 4 Topics:

Infectious diseases · Nutrition and transport in plants · Organisms and their environment · Molecular genetics · Reproduction in plants and humans · Inheritance
