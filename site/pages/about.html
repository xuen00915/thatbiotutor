Success to us means truly helping students: clearer concepts, stronger exam skills, and the confidence to see what they are capable of.

Why we exist

Our Mission

There is a severe need among O Level Pure/G3 Biology students in Singapore. Many struggle because they do not receive high-quality, engaging teaching, leaving them feeling lost and discouraged.

Students deserve reliable, accurate instruction tailored to the latest MOE syllabus. ThatBioTutor was created for exactly that purpose, specialising in O Level Pure/G3 Biology since 2021.

We teach proven memorisation techniques, answering skills, and exam-smart habits while helping students find a genuine love for the subject.

Why ThatBioTutor began

A Message From The Founder

Like many students, I struggled with certain subjects and wanted to improve but did not get enough help in school. In JC, an excellent Economics tutor showed me how much easier a subject can become when someone teaches memorisation and answering skills clearly.

I wanted Biology students to have that same transformative experience: enthusiastic, accurate teaching that makes the subject feel conquerable.

Through improving their Biology grades, I want students to see that they can change and improve whatever their starting point may be. They should leave class with more confidence in themselves than when they entered.

— Mr Keefe
