Quality Teaching Only

Quality in teaching is our upmost priority, which we ensure in the following ways:

  • All tutors at TBT go through rigorous screening and testing that can take up to a month before teaching their first formal class.
  • A tutors' coaching session is held weekly, where tutors are mentored by the founder, Mr Keefe.
  • Every class ends with an after-lesson feedback form. During weekly coaching, we review and act upon the feedback weekly to make sure every class is better than the last.

Why ThatBioTutor began

A Message From The Founder

Q: Why did you start ThatBioTutor?

A: Like many of you, I struggled with certain subjects throughout my school journey. I wanted to improve, but didn't get enough help from my teachers. Sometimes they're too busy, and other times… well, let's just say they weren't too enthusiastic about the subject themselves.

In JC, I took up Econs tuition when I realised my grades were falling. That was when I first experienced the power of a truly capable tutor. I learnt how to memorise content easier and how to answer questions better. The subject became so much easier... It even became one of my favourite!

I want to give you that transformative experience as well. Many students are struggling with Bio, and you might be too. But it doesn't have to be that way. I wish to change that by giving high-quality lessons, taught in an enthusiastic and clear manner. Because you guys deserve the best.

Q: Why is it important that students who really want to do well can do so?

A: I’ve experienced what it’s like to always struggle in a subject. It made me feel inadequate, stupid and worthless. I don’t want them to feel that way.

I want them to see that whatever they put their mind to, they can achieve. They can change and improve themselves, wherever their starting point may be. I want them to see that they are inherently highly capable people, increasing their self-confidence and self-worth.

Q: Why is it that students having high self-confidence important to you?

A: I came from a rocky childhood, and I’m not a psychologist, but I guess that was a big reason for my feelings of low self-worth during childhood.

I don’t want my students to feel that way too: that they’re not good enough, not smart enough, or they’re inadequate. I don’t want them to lead defeated lives.

What set me free of that was proving to myself that I can achieve whatever I set my mind to. Whether it was learning how to solve a Rubik’s cube in 15 seconds, building up my body and physique from the scrawny kid I was, or even building this tuition business… each event showed me that we humans are capable of change and improvement.

Through transforming their Bio grades, I want my students to see that they can achieve whatever they set their mind to. I want them to leave our class having a higher self-esteem and confidence in themselves than before they entered.

— Mr Keefe
