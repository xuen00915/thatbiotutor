1. Planting Trees!

Global reforestation

Learning Biology isn't enough. We need to Protect it.

Up till 2025, ThatBioTutor donated a portion of its profits to global reforestation efforts through Team Trees, which partners with the Arbor Day Foundation.

In total, TBT has planted 1982 trees. You can see the record on the Team Trees donor search.

Closer to home

2. SG Environmental Causes

From 2026 onwards, ThatBioTutor will focus its giving efforts on causes closer to home.

Each year, our students will vote on the environmental cause on Giving.sg that matters most to them, and we will make a lump-sum donation to the chosen cause.
