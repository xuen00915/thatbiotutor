IntensiveFix Fees, Format, Topics, and 2026 Schedule

Eight two-hour lessons run over four days in two weeks, with a one-hour break between lessons. Each lesson is a speedrun crash course with a complete content overhaul and question practice across nine Sec 3 topics, from cells to homeostasis.

Fees and class options

Non-TBT students: $110 per 2-hour lesson, for eight lessons in total. Current TBT students: $90 per 2-hour lesson, for eight lessons in total.

Physical classes are recommended. Online attendance by Zoom is available at $5 off per lesson.

Students who enrol in TBT’s 2027 lessons after IntensiveFix receive $160 off their next invoice when they enrol by 15 December 2026. Each non-TBT friend who signs up with you earns a $150 referral bonus.

1pm–6pm on Monday 16 November 2026, Wednesday 18 November 2026, Monday 23 November 2026, and Wednesday 25 November 2026.

Lessons cover Cells, movement of substances, and biological molecules; enzymes and nutrition in humans; transport in humans; respiration; excretion; and homeostasis.

Is IntensiveFix Right for You?

This is for:

  • Students taking O Level Pure/G3 Biology in Sec 3 or Sec 4.
  • Students who feel very lost in the content, or just need a few concepts strengthened.
  • Students who know the content but blank out on OEQs and lose marks anyway.
  • Students who genuinely want to improve in Bio, out of their own drive, not because someone else is forcing them.
  • Students based in Singapore who can attend physical or online classes.

This isn’t for:

  • Students taking Combined Science Biology, not Pure/G3.
  • Students based outside Singapore.
  • Students only joining because their parents are making them, without wanting to improve themselves.
  • Students who aren’t committed to putting in the work required to ace the subject.
