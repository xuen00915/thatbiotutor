    Frequently Asked Questions

    Q: Do you teach physical lessons?

    +

    Yes! Starting 2024, you choose between coming online or physically.

    Q: Do you have hands-on practical sessions?

    +

    Yes, our hands-on practical sessions cover the most commonly tested experiments, so students walk into Paper 3 with the hand-eye coordination and confidence to protect the full 20% of their grade.

    Q: Other than weekly lessons, what are the other perks of being a student here?

    +

    There are a plethora of benefits, including notes, flashcards, lesson clips, extra practice papers, week-long WhatsApp help, snacks when you come for physical lessons... just to name a few!

    ​​

    All these extra perks and resources are free of charge for our students. For more details please watch the video here.

    Q: Do I need to turn on my mic/camera during online lessons?

    +

    It is not compulsory, though we encourage students to respond to questions their tutor poses to the class via the zoom chat!

    Q: Is there a lot of homework?

    +

    The weekly homework consists of a few questions, and is highly manageable (We don't believe in overloading students with homework when they already have enough from school).

    ​

    Homework is also not compulsory to do, although if you do it, you can claim Homework Points for rewards! (e.g. Bubble Tea, Starbucks Gift Card)

    Q: How many students are there in a class?

    +

    The cap for physical class will be around 20-25 students. Don't be daunted by the numbers, try it out yourself in a trial lesson, and see why so many students still love our classes. 

    ​

    We're so confident your child will love the lesson that we offer a 100% money-back guarantee on the trial. If they didn't love it, just let us know and we'll refund you fully — no questions asked.

    Q: What levels do you teach?

    +

    We teach only O Level Pure/G3 Biology, so we can truly specialise and develop the best lessons, teaching methods and resources for our students.

     

    Sec 3s, classes open for you March of each year!

    Q: Do you teach combined bio?

    +

    At the moment, only Pure Bio.

    Q: Do you teach 1-1 tuition?

    +

    Unfortunately, we have too many students to afford the time to teach 1-1. Good news: Our students can ask us questions 1-1 on WhatsApp, all week long!

    Q: But what if I can’t ask all the questions I want in a group class?

    +

    Our students never face this problem because they get week-long WhatsApp help.

     

    We even take the time to voice message them explanations if the question is more complex.

    Q: Are all the classes taught by Mr Keefe?

    No, as we have a small and elite team of tutors who each take classes of their own. 

     

    Rest assured that no matter which timeslot you go to, the content and methods taught is the same across tutors, as we standardise it for every lesson.
